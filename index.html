How it's made

Four steps. Zero shortcuts.

1

Burn the caramel

Pure cane sugar cooked to a deep amber, poured warm into each jar.

2

Temper the custard

Organic yolks whisked with vanilla-steeped milk, strained twice for silk.

3

Steam low & slow

A gentle water bath at 90°C so it sets tender, never rubbery.

4

Chill overnight

Rested 12 hours so the caramel melts into a glossy amber pool.

Our story

Started on a tiny stovetop in 2019.

Kinari began with one dented saucepan, a grandmother's recipe card, and a stubborn belief that pudding deserves better than plastic cups.

Today we still steam every batch by hand before sunrise, using eggs from a farm 40 minutes away and vanilla we import ourselves. Nothing artificial ever touches the pot.

— Mei & the Kinari kitchen
